Two topics carry almost everything after them.

Fractions in Year 7. Negative numbers in Year 8. Ratio, algebra, trigonometry and calculus all sit on top of them, and neither gets formally revisited once the class has moved on.

So a shaky Year 7 becomes a difficult Year 11, and it looks like the student got worse. They did not. The foundation was always thin and the building got taller.

It is not ability. It is sequence, and sequence problems have solutions.
