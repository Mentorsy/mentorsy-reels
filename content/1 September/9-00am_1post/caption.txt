The children who will do well with AI are the ones who can tell when it is wrong.

That is not a technology skill. It is subject knowledge, held firmly enough to push back against a confident sentence.

Which means the answer to 'should my child still learn maths if AI can do it' is yes, and more urgently than before. You cannot audit an answer in a subject you do not understand.

The tool raises the value of knowing things. It does not lower it.
