What to say when the mark comes back low.

'Did you revise?' is a trial, and children defend themselves in trials rather than think. You will get a defence, not information.

Every question in this list points at the paper instead of the child, which is where the useful information is. 'Was it the topic or the timing' in particular separates two completely different problems that produce identical marks.

And 'show me the one you nearly got' is the fastest route back to a child who has stopped talking about it.
