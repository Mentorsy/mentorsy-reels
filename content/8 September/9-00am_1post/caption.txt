Every child should build one thing that other people actually use.

Not a project marked by a teacher. Something small and real - a page, a tool, a bot that does one job for one person who is not them.

The shift happens the first time someone reports a bug. Suddenly the work has consequences outside the classroom, and the child stops asking whether it is finished and starts asking whether it works.

That is the whole difference between studying computing and being able to build.
