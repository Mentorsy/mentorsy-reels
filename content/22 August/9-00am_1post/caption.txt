What to say the night before your child presents.

'Just be confident' asks them to produce a feeling on demand, which nobody can do. It reads as pressure, not support.

What works is specific and physical. Name the part they know best, so they have somewhere solid to start. Tell them nobody can see nerves, because it is true and they assume otherwise. And tell them to go slower than feels right, because speed is what nerves do to a voice and slowing down is the only lever they can actually pull mid-sentence.
