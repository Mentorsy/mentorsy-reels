In coding, getting stuck is the subject. It is not an interruption to it.

Every professional developer spends most of the day with something not working. That is not a sign of failing, it is the actual job, and a child who thinks being stuck means being bad will quit somewhere around week three.

Worth saying out loud early: this is meant to be hard, everyone's code breaks, and finding out why is the part you are learning.
