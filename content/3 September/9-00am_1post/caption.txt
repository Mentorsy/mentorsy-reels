'I don't get it' is rarely about the topic in front of them.

It is almost always about the one before, which was covered fast, mostly understood, and never closed. Then the next topic arrives assuming it was closed, and the confusion compounds silently.

By the time a child says the words out loud, they are usually two or three topics downstream of where the problem started.

Which is why asking 'what are you working on' rarely helps, and asking 'when did this last make sense' almost always does.
