Your child is not shy. They are unrehearsed.

Shyness sounds like a personality, which makes it sound permanent. Unrehearsed is a state, and states change with repetition.

Nobody is fluent the first time they say a sentence out loud in front of people. They are fluent the ninth time.

The gap between those two is not courage. It is practice nobody scheduled.
