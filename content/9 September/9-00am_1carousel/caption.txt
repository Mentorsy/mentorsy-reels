What a good session actually looks like from the outside.

The student talks more than the tutor. That single ratio separates teaching from lecturing, and it is easy to check - just ask your child who did most of the talking.

Something goes wrong on purpose, because a student who has only seen worked examples has never practised the actual skill, which is recovering.

And it ends with something they can do without help. Not something they watched. Something they can do.
