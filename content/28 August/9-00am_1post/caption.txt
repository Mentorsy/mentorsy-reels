Nerves do not make a child quiet. They make a child fast.

And speed is the thing that costs marks, because nobody can follow it and the speaker cannot hear themselves think.

Telling them to slow down does not work under pressure. Marking the pauses on the page does, because it turns a feeling into an instruction.

The first line matters most. Rehearse it until it is automatic, and the rest arrives on a body that has already started.
