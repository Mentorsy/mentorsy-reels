A maths report is an average of averages.

62 percent might be strong algebra and collapsed geometry, or steady mediocrity across everything. Those need opposite responses, and the number cannot tell them apart.

Ask for the question-level breakdown. Most schools have it and rarely send it unprompted.

That one request changes the whole conversation.
