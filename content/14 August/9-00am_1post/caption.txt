Two ways to teach a child to code, and only one of them compounds.

Course completion is easy to measure, which is why it gets measured. But a child who has finished nine tutorials and never debugged anything has not learned to program. They have learned to type.

The skill is what happens after it breaks.

Ask any coding class what a student does in week three when their code does not run. The answer tells you which column you are buying.
