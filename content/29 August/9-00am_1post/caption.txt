The first session should be diagnosis, not chapter one.

Starting the syllabus on day one assumes the problem is coverage. It almost never is. It is usually one unfinished topic somewhere behind, quietly making everything built on top of it harder than it should be.

Nobody can teach a gap they have not located.

Ask any tutor what they will do in the first hour. The answer tells you most of what you need to know.
