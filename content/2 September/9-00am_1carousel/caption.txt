Three questions worth asking on a school tour.

The brochure will not answer any of them, and the person walking you round may need to go and find out. That is useful information too.

The third one matters most. A school that puts its strongest teacher with the students who are struggling believes ability can move. A school that gives the bottom set to whoever is available has already decided it cannot.

You will learn more from that answer than from the entire prospectus.
