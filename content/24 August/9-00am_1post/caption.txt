Your child used AI for their homework. The ban does not work.

It only moves the behaviour somewhere you cannot see, and removes your ability to teach anything about it.

What works is asking them to explain the work without the screen. The gap shows up in about ninety seconds, and it shows up to them, not just to you. That is a far better lesson than a rule.

Then agree what the tool is allowed to do next time. Explain a concept, yes. Write the paragraph, no. Children keep rules they helped write.
