Four questions that reveal whether a child understands science or has memorised it.

None of them ask for a definition. A definition can be recited by a child who has understood nothing, which is why exams stopped rewarding them.

These four ask what the idea is connected to. Understanding is a shape, not a fact, and you can only see the shape from the edges.

Try one tonight over dinner. Two minutes, no marking, and you will know more than the last report told you.
