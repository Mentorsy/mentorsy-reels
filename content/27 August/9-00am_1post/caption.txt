Three things decide a French grade, and vocabulary is not one of them.

Examiners are not counting words. They are checking whether a student can hold a tense across a whole answer, give an opinion and justify it, and keep going when a word will not come.

That third one is worth practising deliberately. Every candidate loses a word mid-sentence. The mark difference is whether they stop dead or say it another way.

There is a phrase for that in every language. Teach the phrase.
