What to actually teach a child about AI.

Not prompting. Prompting will be obsolete in two years and it was never the hard part.

The durable skill is judgement: knowing when the answer is wrong, where it came from, and when the tool should stay closed. A child who can spot a confident wrong answer is more employable than one who can write a clever prompt.

That skill is just critical thinking with a new surface. Which is good news, because we already know how to teach it.
