Speaking is the only school subject that is also every subsequent interview.

And almost nobody trains for it, because it does not appear on a report card. It appears at nineteen, in a room, when the grades have already done their job and something else is being assessed.

The useful thing about it is that it responds to practice faster than almost anything else on a timetable. Four weeks of deliberate work changes how a child sounds. Four weeks of maths rarely changes a grade.

Start earlier than feels necessary.
