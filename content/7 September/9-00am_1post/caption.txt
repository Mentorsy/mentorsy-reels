Practicals carry more weight than their share of the marks suggests.

Method questions appear across every paper, and they are answered well by students who have actually stood over an experiment that did not work. You can revise a method. You cannot revise the instinct for what usually goes wrong.

There is a second reason. Ten years later, the mole calculations are gone and the afternoon the reaction turned an unexpected colour is still there.

A subject people remember is a subject people carry on with.
