Two ways to revise a language, and only one of them builds retrieval.

Everything in the left column feels productive because it is fluent and comfortable. Nothing is difficult, so nothing is being strengthened.

Everything in the right column feels worse and works better. That is the whole principle: memory grows at the point of effortful recall, not at the point of recognition.

A student who re-reads a list four times will recognise every word and produce none of them.
