How to tell a real coding class from an expensive one.

Ask to see a student project from week eight, not the showreel. Week eight is where the difference lives: either everyone's project looks identical, which means they copied, or they look different, which means they built.

Ask what happens when a child finishes early. In a good class, they extend it. In a weak one, they wait.

And ask how many students share a screen. Two is fine, and often better. Four means three are watching.
