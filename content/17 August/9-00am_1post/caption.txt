Why a child can score well in French and still freeze in Paris.

Written exams reward recall. Speaking requires retrieval under time pressure, which is a different skill using a different part of the memory, and almost nothing in a school term practises it.

A vocabulary list learned on Sunday and tested on Friday is stored, not owned.

Ask when your child last had to answer a question in French without knowing it was coming.
